Explorer · Open access in Africa · 2002–2021

Almost half of indexed African research is free to read.

We matched articles with an African author address (Web of Science, 2002–2021) to their open access status in Unpaywall, measured in August 2022. Use the filters to explore by year, country, research area, collaboration and funding.

How to read and reuse

What the data can and cannot say

What is counted

Articles in the Web of Science Core Collection with at least one African institutional address, a DOI and a publication year 2002–2021. Open access status is one snapshot from Unpaywall, taken in August 2022.

Types of open access

  • Gold: in a fully open journal
  • Hybrid: open, with a licence, in a subscription journal
  • Bronze: free on the publisher's site, no licence
  • Green: free only in a repository

Main limits

Web of Science covers journals published in Africa poorly, so these numbers describe the internationally visible part of African research. All comparisons are descriptive. Unpaywall changed its method in May 2025, so a new query would give slightly different values.
